StreamClock

Last updated: 5 September 2026

StreamClock is a black-background overlay clock for streaming, recording and live production. It can stay on top of other windows and includes a stopwatch, external time sync (system clock / NTP), and timecode output (LTC over audio, MTC over MIDI).

Main features

  • Time sources (system clock / NTP)
  • LTC (SMPTE Linear Timecode) output over audio
  • MTC (MIDI Time Code) output through a MIDI port
  • Frame rates: 23.976 / 24 / 25 / 29.97DF / 29.97 / 30
  • Stopwatch
  • Always on top, transparent background and customisable text colour
  • Can also hide the clock and operate as a time-source-to-LTC/MTC converter

Pricing

StreamClock is free. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. No purchase is required, so there is nothing to cancel. It does not appear in the iOS/macOS “Subscriptions” list.
